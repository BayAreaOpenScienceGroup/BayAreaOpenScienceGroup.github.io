October 27, 2026 - Zoom Registration

Meet MySuppleHub: An Open, Community-Contributed Evidence Base for Dietary Supplements

This month we are joined by Kelly Yoo and Meenu Krishnakumar from Stanford, who will discuss their work on MySuppleHub. Over half of US adults use dietary supplements, yet the evidence supporting many products remains fragmented, uneven in quality, and often influenced by industry funding. MySuppleHub, developed by the Snyder Lab in the Department of Genetics at Stanford, approaches this problem as an open science opportunity. The platform combines a citation-linked supplement encyclopedia with community surveys, polls, and self-reported experiences, creating a shared evidence base in which participants can both contribute their experiences and benefit from a growing body of accessible, evidence-informed information.

December 1, 2026 - Zoom Registration coming

OpenADMET - Raising the bar on ADMET prediction with open science

Drug discovery programs frequently stall due to the Avoid-ome, which comprises the complex off-target interactions and ADMET liabilities that traditional heuristic methods struggle to address. To solve this, OpenADMET combines high-resolution structural biology, directed data generation, and machine learning into a systematic framework for predicting and mitigating these liabilities. Central to this mission is generating targeted datasets to host large-scale blind benchmarks that test community predictive models on real-world drug design problems. By uniting hundreds of academic and industry teams through these challenges, webinars, and conferences, the initiative fosters widespread collaboration to establish transparent, data-driven standards across the field.
Pat Walters is Chief Scientist at OpenADMET, an open-science initiative that integrates high-throughput experimentation, structural biology, and machine learning to advance ADMET prediction. Alongside his work at OpenADMET, he serves as an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Bioengineering & Therapeutic Sciences at UCSF. Pat brings over thirty years of industry leadership to his roles, having previously held senior positions at Relay Therapeutics and Vertex Pharmaceuticals. A dedicated educator and communicator, he is the author of the widely read blog “Practical Cheminformatics,” where he shares technical insights on computational drug discovery.
